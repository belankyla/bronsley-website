Who We Are

About Bronsley Community Resource Center

Bronsley Community Resource Center is dedicated to supporting and empowering young people in Los Angeles through collaboration with local schools, organizations, and volunteers. Our center hosts monthly community grocery giveaways and maintains our Rooted & Rising urban community farm.

Our team is passionate about our mission and the positive impact we strive to create. We are grateful for the support of our volunteers, donors, and community partners who make this work possible.

We believe every young person deserves access to mentorship, nutritious food, and the skills they need to thrive. Join us in creating a brighter future for the next generation.

A Heart for the Community

The founder of Bronsley Community Resource Center grew up witnessing the inequities faced by young people in Los Angeles — unequal access to mentorship, nutritious food, and safe spaces to grow. Driven by a deep belief that every child deserves the opportunity to thrive, she set out to build something lasting in the heart of the community she loves.

With a background in community organizing, youth advocacy, and nonprofit leadership, she launched Bronsley in 2024 with a clear vision: create a center where Los Angeles youth could access real resources, real mentors, and real hope. In just one year, Bronsley has served over 5,000 people across the greater LA area.

From organizing monthly grocery giveaways to building the Rooted & Rising urban farm that reconnects youth with the land, the founder's heart is in every program Bronsley offers. She believes in meeting people where they are, listening before acting, and building bridges across schools, families, and organizations.

"This community gave me everything. Bronsley is my way of giving it back — not just for today, but for every generation that comes after us."

Why We Exist

Our Mission Statement

"To empower youth and enrich the Los Angeles community by providing access to mentorship, essential resources, and safe spaces where every person can grow, learn, serve, and succeed."

Bronsley exists because we believe in the dignity and potential of every young person in our city. We are not just a resource center — we are a movement built on love, accountability, and community.

We measure our success in lives transformed — in the child who finds a mentor, the family that eats well this week, and the teenager who grows their first tomato in our Rooted & Rising farm.

What We Offer

Youth Programs

All Bronsley programs are free, community-driven, and designed to meet youth where they are.

Ages 6–12 · After School
Homework Help & Tutoring

Certified tutors and volunteer mentors provide weekly academic support in reading, math, and science — in partnership with local schools.

Ages 13–18 · Saturdays
Youth Leadership Academy

A semester-long leadership program teaching public speaking, civic engagement, financial literacy, and community organizing. Graduates receive a certificate and mentorship stipend.

Ages 10–18 · Year-Round
Rooted & Rising Farm Program

Youth plant, tend, and harvest fresh produce that feeds our community. Combines environmental education with food justice work.

Ages 14–24 · Ongoing
Mentorship & Career Pathways

One-on-one mentoring with professionals in healthcare, tech, education, and trades. Includes job shadowing and internship connections.

All Ages · Monthly
Arts & Creative Expression

Monthly workshops in visual art, spoken word, music, and photography — a safe space for youth to discover their creative voice.

Families · Monthly
Community Food Giveaway

Every 3rd Saturday, youth volunteers help distribute essential groceries and connect neighbors to Bronsley resources.

About the Farm

Growing More Than Food

The Rooted & Rising Farm is Bronsley's urban community farm — a hands-on space where youth ages 10–18 learn to plant, tend, and harvest fresh fruits, vegetables, and herbs. All produce is grown organically and distributed free at our monthly food giveaway.

More than agriculture, the farm is a space for healing, connection, and education. Young people learn about food justice, environmental stewardship, nutrition, and the deep relationship between land and community wellness.

Youth who complete the full growing season earn a Rooted & Rising certificate and are invited back as junior farm leaders the following year.
